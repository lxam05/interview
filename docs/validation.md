# Validation protocol

Nothing here has been measured yet. This is the plan for finding out whether
the product is useful, with the numbers that decide what happens next.

## Question

Does a short, sourced brief change how an Irish CS student prepares for a specific internship interview, and do they trust it?

## Stage 1: evidence exists (before any students)

Run research for 8 to 10 candidate internships chosen by how much public evidence turns up.

- **Go** if at least 3 internships produce a brief a reviewer would publish (at least 3 topics with "single report" or stronger, and at least one source dated in the last 24 months).
- **Measure** on about 20 hand-labelled documents: extraction precision, dropped-quote rate, intern/graduate and Ireland/international classification accuracy, cost and time per run.
- **Stop and rethink** if fewer than 3 briefs are publishable: lean on student reports first (societies, careers offices) before more scraping.

## Stage 2: five student interviews

Show 5 students who have an interview coming up the brief for their company. Watch, don't explain. Ask:

1. What would you study first, and why?
2. Which part would you not trust? Why?
3. What is missing that you would have looked for elsewhere?

- **Go** if at least 4 of 5 can name what to study first from the brief alone, and at least 3 say they would use it again.
- **Fix first** if more than 2 misread the evidence labels.

## Stage 3: first 100 users

Channels, in order of expected yield:

1. Computing and engineering societies at TCD, UCD, DCU, UL, UCC, University of Galway and Maynooth: one post per society with a brief for a company their members apply to.
2. Careers offices: a one-page explainer for the people who run internship briefings.
3. Student Discords and the r/ireland and r/cscareerquestionsEU communities, posting a useful brief, not an ad, and following each community's rules.
4. Everyone who sends a report gets their organised report back; ask them to pass the page to one friend.

Track per week: searches, brief views, reports sent, reports approved, error reports.

- **Go** (keep building) if within 6 weeks: 100 students have viewed a brief, at least 15 reports are sent and at least 10 approved, and at least 3 new internships reach a publishable brief from reports.
- **Stop or pivot** if fewer than 5 reports arrive in 6 weeks: students will not contribute, so the evidence base will not grow.
